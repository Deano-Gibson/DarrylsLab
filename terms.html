How enquiries and personal training bookings work.

Updated 28 September 2026 · Version enquiry-2026-09-28-v2

Who you're contacting

I'm Darryl Labuschagne, your coach at DL Coaching. You can contact me at darryllabuschagne14@gmail.com about your enquiry, a session or a concern.

Your enquiry and booking

Submitting the form lets me review your goals and contact you to arrange a call. It does not reserve a time, confirm a session, create a paid contract or give you access to the booking portal. I'll agree the location, time, session details and any applicable price with you before you commit to a booking.

Your free first session

Your first personal training session is free. We'll arrange it together after discussing your goals and completing the necessary pre-exercise screening. You do not have to buy a package to send an enquiry or take up your free first session.

Prices and payment

No payment is taken through this enquiry form. If you decide to continue with paid training, I'll confirm the price, what's included and how to pay before you agree. Sending an enquiry does not commit you to paid sessions.

Training information and screening

Please provide accurate information and tell me about changes that may affect your training. The enquiry form does not replace pre-exercise health screening. If you haven't completed screening, I'll discuss the next step with you before training. You can leave the optional health-information field blank and discuss any concerns with me directly.

Personal training is not medical diagnosis or treatment. I'll discuss whether training is suitable for your needs and whether professional medical guidance is needed before proceeding. Please tell me if an exercise causes pain or makes you feel unwell.

Changing or cancelling a session

Contact me as soon as possible if you need to change or cancel a session. Any cancellation or rescheduling policy for paid training will be provided before you agree to that booking. This enquiry form does not impose a cancellation fee. If I need to change a session, I'll contact you to agree what happens next.

Your information

Darrylslab.com stores and uses your contact details and answers to review your enquiry, follow up and prepare for our conversation. Optional health information is used to understand your training needs. Contact me to ask about your information, request its deletion or withdraw your consent to processing optional health information. Withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before it. Please do not submit someone else's private details.

Service and your rights

I'll provide coaching with reasonable care and skill. Progress varies from person to person, and I do not promise a specific result. Nothing in these terms takes away your statutory consumer rights or excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded, including liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence.

Questions or concerns

Contact me so we can discuss and resolve any concerns. These enquiry terms do not replace any additional session details or policies we agree before a booking. Future updates will not retrospectively change an existing agreement.
