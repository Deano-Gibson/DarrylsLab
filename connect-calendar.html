Use your own Google account. This connection is reserved for you.

You sign in directly with Google. DL Coaching never sees your password. Your saved connection is encrypted and stays on the server.

What you’re allowing

Google will ask for permission to view availability and manage events on calendars you own. DL Coaching uses this to check for clashes and add training bookings. Your Google email is used to verify that it’s you.

Booking times use UK local time, including daylight-saving changes. Your published training hours are managed separately.

You can remove access in your Google account at any time. If access expires or is removed, return here to reconnect.
